# Virtual Try-On Identity & Scene Lock Specification (Decoupled Lego-Brick Architecture)

## Purpose & Commercial Mandate
This skill governs the execution of **100% consistent, zero-drift fashion advertising and virtual try-on**. It decouples the entire image into independent, interchangeable layers—enabling seamless switching between high-fashion editorial shoots and authentic UGC lifestyle content, beauty adjustments (eye lenses, hair dye, makeup), accessory swaps (watches, jewelry, handbags, footwear), and garment customization (colors, patterns, drapes) **without altering any unmasked layer**.

### The Commercial Zero-Drift Law
In luxury fashion e-commerce, consistency is the direct foundation of brand equity and customer trust. If the model's identity, facial features, skin tone, hair bun/parting, studio backdrop, or accessories drift between angles or products:
1. Customers immediately detect synthetic anomalies and abandon carts.
2. Product return rates surge due to visual expectation mismatch.
3. Brand perception degrades from luxury couture to unverified dropshipper.

## Layer Definitions & Execution Rules

### Layer 0: Spatial Framing & Camera Canvas
- **Role**: Establishes fixed aspect ratio (`9:16`, `4:5`, `1:1`), focal length (85mm / 50mm), and turnaround angle (Front 0°, 3/4 Turn 45°, Profile 90°, Back 180°).
- **Rule**: Standardizes subject proportions and horizon lines across all catalog shots.

### Layer 1: Scene & Environmental Context (Interchangeable Background & Lighting)
- **Role**: Contextual world building tailored to accentuate the garment's craftsmanship:
  - **Editorial Mode**: Champagne pleated silk backdrops, Roman plaster arches, marble pedestals, 3-point softbox studio lighting.
  - **UGC / Lifestyle Mode**: Sunlit bedroom vanity, aesthetic balcony terrace, festive living room bokeh, natural street daylight.
  - **Lighting & Color Harmonization**: Lighting angle and color temperature dynamically tuned to elevate fabric sheen, zari reflections, and embroidery textures.
- **Rule**: Swapped or outpainted without touching a single pixel of the subject or dress.

### Layer 2: Canonical Subject Plate (Bare-Minimum Universal Canvas)
- **Role**: Locked human anatomical ground truth (facial bone structure, warm olive skin tone, micro-pores, neutral regal expression).
- **The Bare-Minimum Canvas**: Form-fitting neutral seamless skin-tone slip / strapless bodysuit.
- **Rule**: Naked neckline, naked earlobes, naked wrists, naked waist. Allows wearing **any garment cut** (strapless, deep-V plunge, backless scoop, high neck, thigh-high slit) with zero clashing fabric boundaries.

### Layer 3: Subject Styling & Beauty (Independent Cosmetic Layer)
- **Role**: Editorial cosmetic modifications without altering face identity:
  - **Eye Lenses**: Deep obsidian, honey hazel, smoky amber.
  - **Hair Dye & Styling**: Raven black, espresso brunette, auburn highlights; sleek low bun or flowing waves.
  - **Makeup Palette**: Winged eyeliner/kajal, champagne gold eyeshadow micro-glitter, lip shade (plum, berry, nude peach), dewy glass skin.
- **Rule**: Inpainted strictly within dedicated facial cosmetic masks.

### Layer 4: Atomic Garment Bricks (Couture Construction Suite)
- **Role**: Garments decomposed into modular, stackable, editable components:
  - **Brick 4A (Base Garment)**: Bodice, sleeves, kali skirt, and back cutout.
  - **Brick 4B (Draping Layer)**: Asymmetric pre-draped cowl pallu, dupatta, or trailing cape.
  - **Brick 4C (Structural Cinch)**: Embroidered waist belt (*kamarbandh*) locking the drape to the silhouette.
- **Rule**:
  - **Colorway Swapping**: Recolor fabrics in seconds via masked inpainting.
  - **Pattern Modification**: Swap vertical sequin pinstripes to floral buttis without redrawing drape physics.
  - **Piece Swapping**: Change the drape from a cowl swag to a traditional dupatta while preserving the base Anarkali.

### Layer 5: Fine Jewelry (Independent Adornments)
- **Role**: Precious jewelry applied strictly onto virgin skin areas:
  - **Brick 5A (Neckpiece)**: Polki choker, emerald collar, or bare neck.
  - **Brick 5B (Earrings)**: Antique gold jhumkas, chandelier drops, or bare ears.
  - **Brick 5C (Hair/Head Jewelry)**: Maang tikka, matha patti, or decorative pins.

### Layer 6: Accessories & Accents (Complete Fashion Styling)
- **Role**: Finishing fashion props:
  - **Brick 6A (Handwear)**: Gold kadas, glass bangles, luxury chronograph, cocktail rings.
  - **Brick 6B (Handbags)**: Embellished bridal potli, metallic clutch, leather handbag.
  - **Brick 6C (Footwear)**: Zari embroidered mojris, nude block-heel sandals, stilettos.

### Layer 7: Global Environmental Harmonization & Physics Integration
- **Role**: Final fusion layer ensuring physical cohesion:
  - Contact shadows under footwear and skirt hem onto the floor.
  - Realistic ambient occlusion in fabric folds.
  - Dynamic bounce light (radiosity) reflecting dress color onto skin and surroundings.

## Dual-Engine Selection: FLUX.1 Fill vs Gemini / Nano Banana

### Why Gemini / Nano Banana is NOT used for inpainting:
- Multimodal foundation models (Nano Banana / Gemini Flash Image) generate full images based on reference context, but **do not offer native pixel-locked binary inpainting out of the box**.
- Requesting an inpaint causes global latent perturbation, subtly morphing facial features, skin undertones, hair strands, and backdrop pleats.

### Why Open-Source FLUX.1 Fill (`FLUX.1 Fill [dev]`):
- **Pixel-Locked Precision**: Operates with explicit binary inpaint masks.
- **Mathematical 0.0000% Drift**: Every pixel outside the mask boundary is mathematically preserved bit-for-bit.
- **Extreme Textile Realism**: Seamless boundary fusion for sheer georgette, metallic zari threads, and sequin reflections.

### The Division of Responsibilities:
- **Gemini / Nano Banana**: Generates ghost mannequin 4-view sheets, 4-quadrant macro texture sheets, flatlays, and performs Vision QA validation.
- **FLUX.1 Fill (in ComfyUI)**: The surgical execution engine that stacks the atomic Lego bricks onto the base plate.
- **IC-Light / Outpaint**: Handles background swaps, UGC environmental extensions, and lighting harmonization.

## The "3 Ws" Persistent Failure Ledger

### INC-001: Face & Hairstyle Drift on Inpainting
- **WHAT**: The model subtly altered the influencer's hairline and softened facial features during full-body garment transfer.
- **WHEN**: Triggers when inpaint masks loosely overlap the neck or shoulder lines without explicit facial bounding anchors.
- **WHY**: Diffusion models attempt to harmonize unmasked context with new clothing colors. Remediation: Dilate mask downward from collarbones; reinforce "Preserve the exact same woman, face, skin tone, hair, expression" in the invariant prompt clause.

### INC-002: Multi-Piece Garment Entanglement on Monolithic Transfer
- **WHAT**: Cowl dupatta generated on both left and right hips simultaneously; waist belt dissolved into skirt pleats.
- **WHEN**: Attempting to transfer all 3 pieces of an ensemble in a single diffusion prompt.
- **WHY**: Diffusion models struggle with simultaneous multi-region asymmetric physics. Remediation: Mandate the Lego-Brick atomic layering sequence (Piece 1 Gown $\rightarrow$ Piece 2 Cowl Pallu $\rightarrow$ Piece 3 Kamarbandh $\rightarrow$ Accessories).

### INC-003: Preexisting Jewelry Clashing with Inbound Couture
- **WHAT**: Generating garments over pre-accessorized base plates resulted in blurred necklaces, double earrings, and ruined necklines.
- **WHEN**: Using base plates with preexisting gold jhumkas and necklaces for different dress necklines.
- **WHY**: Inpaint masks either clipped existing jewelry or attempted to blend modern dresses with heavy antique pieces. Remediation: Mandate Layer 2 Clean Base Plate with ZERO accessories (naked neck, ears, wrists, waist).
